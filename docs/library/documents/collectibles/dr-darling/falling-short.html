Falling Short

Dr. Darling's update for Trench on the progress of Dylan's training.

Trench, you wanted an update on Dylan. Well, you can tell he’s about to turn thirteen. Testing boundaries, questioning everything. It’s hard for him to accept that he’s too dangerous to make his own decisions, that we can’t afford for him to make mistakes.

And frankly, he’s falling short of expectations. He has the potential, no question. But activating it. Maybe it’s that we just... we still don’t understand the nature of his abilities or... he’s simply holding back.

He’s too closed off. His parents’ neglect did a lot of damage. Bureau psychiatrists think he’s looking at me as a type of father figure now. We’re all aware how much the boy has lost but you know me. I never wanted children and I would be awful at it if I tried.

So, starting next week, we’ll drop the "buddy" approach and try something else. I’ll still be in the picture, but we’ll start pushing it. Setting hard limits. Contact with project staff only.

Dylan doesn’t yet understand what’s at stake here. It’s time he starts growing up.

Trench. Zachariah, you may not want to hear this but, I am worried about you. Maybe you should take some time off. Dylan has a long way to go before you can step down.
