Wide Edge

Combo Finisher Form

A powerful, wide attack that deals massive Damage and pushes enemies away.

Unlock Living Shard 1
Unlock Aberrant Strength Boost +5
Damage Type Raw Damage
Power Recovery High
Critical Chance 0%
