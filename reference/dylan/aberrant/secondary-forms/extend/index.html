Extend

Secondary Aberrant Form

A long range attack that may hit multiple enemies.

Charge for Falter and extra Damage - a perfect charge deals more.

Unlock Living Shard 2
Unlock Aberrant Strength Boost +5

Power Recovery Medium
Critical Chance 0%
