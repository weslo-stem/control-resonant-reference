Barrage

Combat Ability

Sends projectiles hurtling towards a target.

Obtained by defeating The Artist
Damage Type Blunt Damage
Power cost 400


Hold to increase number of projectiles (up to 3). Inherits Fire Damage boost and Falter boost from Ignite. Damage and falter values will be increased by talents and status buffs. Below is a table of the base damage and falter values per Ability Strength level.
