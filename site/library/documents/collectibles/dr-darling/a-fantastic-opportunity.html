A Fantastic Opportunity

Dr. Darling's initial log regarding the Ordinary AWE and the discovery of the Faden siblings.

Just back from Ordinary, Maine. What a mess! We found an orphan. Dylan Faden, age 10, parents, well, we’re still assessing, but almost certainly lost in the AWE.

It's an awful, awful tragedy. But, also a fantastic opportunity, for the Bureau, and for Dylan. He’s a very bright, very interesting boy. A lot of signs point to possible paranatural ability. Maybe even on levels we’ve never seen before. We’re starting initial evaluations now.

Also, of note: Dylan’s older sister Jesse, current whereabouts unknown. It would be unusual for two siblings both to be strong parautilitarians -- Persons of Power -- but there’s a chance, more than a chance. I think it’s likely. Ideally, we would have access to the sister too, but--

If everything works out with Dylan, we would take him on permanently. Raise him as a Prime Candidate right here in the Oldest House. Imagine the potential just waiting to be tapped! And the great things we could learn from him too.

But I'm getting way ahead of myself!

The point is: this kid could be going places.
