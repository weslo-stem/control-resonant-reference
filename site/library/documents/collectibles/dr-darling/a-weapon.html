A Weapon

After a breakthrough, Dr. Darling reconsiders Dylan's role in the FBC.

We can now conclusively say that as far as Objects of Power go, Dylan is quite useless.

But he just demonstrated something else. Something wholly unprecedented.

We think Dylan built a connection, for lack of a better word, to another place. I don’t want to speculate as to where, but preliminary data suggests it was extradimensional. We captured very grainy, very tentative footage. A void of sorts, with a dark sky. This opens up countless possibilities! These incredible investments of time and resources in Dylan, years of effort, they can still pay off! Can Dylan become the Director without Objects of Power? That’s uncertain. But maybe we don’t need a director. Maybe we need a weapon. The point is: we can still use him. We can make this work.

We can make this work.

Trench. Trench is changing, a little bit every year. I fear he’s been compromised somehow, under an influence we do not yet understand. He won’t let me do anymore tests, but I don’t need to. This is frankly above my paygrade. The Board has done nothing. And so it falls to Dylan. At least... At least now I know he has the power.

I just need to force it out of him.

On an unrelated note, today marks a milestone of sorts. I just found out my father died.
