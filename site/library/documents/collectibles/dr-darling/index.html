Dr. Darling

(1/19)

A Fantastic Opportunity

Dr. Darling's initial log regarding the Ordinary AWE and the discovery of the Faden siblings.

Just back from Ordinary, Maine. What a mess! We found an orphan. Dylan Faden, age 10, parents, well, we’re still assessing, but almost certainly lost in the AWE.

It's an awful, awful tragedy. But, also a fantastic opportunity, for the Bureau, and for Dylan. He’s a very bright, very interesting boy. A lot of signs point to possible paranatural ability. Maybe even on levels we’ve never seen before. We’re starting initial evaluations now.

Also, of note: Dylan’s older sister Jesse, current whereabouts unknown. It would be unusual for two siblings both to be strong parautilitarians -- Persons of Power -- but there’s a chance, more than a chance. I think it’s likely. Ideally, we would have access to the sister too, but--

If everything works out with Dylan, we would take him on permanently. Raise him as a Prime Candidate right here in the Oldest House. Imagine the potential just waiting to be tapped! And the great things we could learn from him too.

But I'm getting way ahead of myself!

The point is: this kid could be going places.

(2/19)

Falling Short

Dr. Darling's update for Trench on the progress of Dylan's training.

Trench, you wanted an update on Dylan. Well, you can tell he’s about to turn thirteen. Testing boundaries, questioning everything. It’s hard for him to accept that he’s too dangerous to make his own decisions, that we can’t afford for him to make mistakes.

And frankly, he’s falling short of expectations. He has the potential, no question. But activating it. Maybe it’s that we just... we still don’t understand the nature of his abilities or... he’s simply holding back.

He’s too closed off. His parents’ neglect did a lot of damage. Bureau psychiatrists think he’s looking at me as a type of father figure now. We’re all aware how much the boy has lost but you know me. I never wanted children and I would be awful at it if I tried.

So, starting next week, we’ll drop the "buddy" approach and try something else. I’ll still be in the picture, but we’ll start pushing it. Setting hard limits. Contact with project staff only.

Dylan doesn’t yet understand what’s at stake here. It’s time he starts growing up.

Trench. Zachariah, you may not want to hear this but, I am worried about you. Maybe you should take some time off. Dylan has a long way to go before you can step down.

(3/19)

Perfect

A recording from one of Dylan's training sessions.

No. No, we’ve been through this. Protocol changed. You have to push him. You have to.

Abort. You’re there to motivate P6, not coddle him. He needs to be at the correct level of agitation. Frustrated, angry, sad, hungry, sleep-deprived. Look, I don’t care which one it is. Just make it work. Now, reset and go back to one.

Dylan, we are running out of time. Do you understand that? Trench is--

There are forces at work you cannot comprehend. Threats that could destroy everything we have here. You cannot make mistakes. You have to be perfect. You need--

You don’t. I had to put aside important work to supervise you, because you won’t listen to staff. Now go run a hundred laps while I talk to your instructor. Please.

You have to keep him in the right mindset. You call the tune. You set the pace. You know how important our work here is.

We can’t afford for him to have limits.

Well, my father was a disciplinarian and I turned out fine. Unless he’s got a broken bone, testing continues.

Fine, let’s go up to 150 milligrams. If he gets sluggish, we’ll try something else. There’s a difference between “pliable” and “zombie.”

Dylan? No, no, don’t stop, just listen. If this was easy, anybody could do it.

Enough complaining. Finish your laps, then take your meds, and listen to your instructor!

Goddammit.

(4/19)

A Weapon

After a breakthrough, Dr. Darling reconsiders Dylan's role in the FBC.

We can now conclusively say that as far as Objects of Power go, Dylan is quite useless.

But he just demonstrated something else. Something wholly unprecedented.

We think Dylan built a connection, for lack of a better word, to another place. I don’t want to speculate as to where, but preliminary data suggests it was extradimensional. We captured very grainy, very tentative footage. A void of sorts, with a dark sky. This opens up countless possibilities! These incredible investments of time and resources in Dylan, years of effort, they can still pay off! Can Dylan become the Director without Objects of Power? That’s uncertain. But maybe we don’t need a director. Maybe we need a weapon. The point is: we can still use him. We can make this work.

We can make this work.

Trench. Trench is changing, a little bit every year. I fear he’s been compromised somehow, under an influence we do not yet understand. He won’t let me do anymore tests, but I don’t need to. This is frankly above my paygrade. The Board has done nothing. And so it falls to Dylan. At least... At least now I know he has the power.

I just need to force it out of him.

On an unrelated note, today marks a milestone of sorts. I just found out my father died.

(5/19)

Volatile Paranatural Entity

An update from Dr. Darling to his staff.

Are you ready? I'm ready to go. Are you ready? Are you ready? Alright. I need, I need, I need you to be ready. I'm ready--

Staff security update. As you all know, Dylan made a third attempt to leave our care. This incident led to a number of injuries. It was entirely avoidable. More importantly, he almost made it out.

He almost. Made it out. We are in the middle of Manhattan. This is not a theoretical threat.

I know one of you has been sneaking Dylan into the cafeteria for ice cream. Ice cream! We are running a multi-million dollar government training program. This is not a school. We are not teachers. Dylan is not a normal teenager. He is a volatile, paranatural entity, and an FBC asset. If he gets out into the city, he could hurt someone. He could hurt a lot of someones.

I know what you’re thinking: “What could be so dangerous about a little mint chocolate chip?” But you’re looking at this the wrong way. Imagine taking a vial of nitroglycerin out with you to an ice cream social. You so much as trip, and... kablammo!

We will be making some changes around here. I’ve transferred a new employee to the program: Roberts. He’ll be Dylan’s warden, in charge of his security. It’ll be 24/7 surveillance from now on. For Dylan’s safety as much as our own. He is not to leave the Prime Candidate area at all, ever, for any reason.

The stakes are way too high for sloppy work now. You heard Trench yesterday. He’s saying things that don’t make sense. “A copy of a copy of a copy”... “orange peel”... I mean...

Dylan is our only chance. If he fails when Trench is compromised and we run into something we can't fight.

Kablammo.

Cut.

I can’t send this to them. Can we... I'm trying to get... I'm trying to tell the staff something here. To help -- you've gotta help...

(6/19)

Smart Boy

Dr. Darling's video welcoming Dylan to the Prime Candidate Program.

Hi, Dylan, it’s me, your buddy, Dr. Casper Darling. I can’t be there today because I am very busy on an important expedition. But I still wanted to be here to welcome you to your first official day of training. You’ll be under my tutelage at the Oldest House. Don’t worry, it’ll soon feel like home. Now, I’m going to tell you a little bit about the FBC.

“FBC” stands for Federal Bureau of Control. We’re a little bit like the FBI or CIA, except we deal with AWEs or “Altered World Events”, like the one that took place in your hometown. Now, our mission is to study, contain, and control paranatural phenomena.

Remember Trench? He's the Director of the FBC. Big man in charge. Now, you won’t see much of him around here because he’s busy using his special powers to keep the Bureau safe from all sorts of paranatural, extradimensional threats, and running the day to day here at the FBC.

Now, I know you’ve already figured this out, Dylan, because you are a smart boy: we think you have some special powers of your very own. At the FBC, we have a program dedicated to unique individuals like yourself: The Prime Candidate Program. We’re going to unlock your true potential here. You just have to do exactly as we say at all times. You saw what happened in your hometown. As far as we know, it wasn’t your fault, at least not directly, but nonetheless, that’s a good example of what can happen when paranatural power goes unchecked.

But don’t worry about that now! We're gonna have a lotta of fun together!

You are going to be a real asset to the FBC, Dylan.

(7/19)

Extraordinary Individual

Dr. Darling's "welcome" video for FBC staff joining the Prime Candidate Program.

Welcome to the Prime Candidate Program. Staff orientation begins now. Get ready to leap into the unknown! Don’t be frightened -- I mean, rest assured that you have been chosen for your skills, dedication, and especially your discretion. So, if you haven’t signed the program NDA yet, turn around, you’re in the wrong place.

But for the rest of you: a little context. In some ways, the Federal Bureau of Control is a government agency like any other.

Now, as you know, directors of similar agencies typically serve by presidential appointment.

However, the Director of the FBC is appointed through another, classified process, one we do not yet fully understand.

But, what we do know, is that a hopeful Director must successfully bind the Service Weapon. A very important Object of Power. If the candidate fails, the result is death.

The Prime Candidate Program aims to provide, in the event of a change in leadership, a candidate qualified to take the reins of the FBC and continue our tradition of excellence. Prime Candidates must possess paranatural abilities, yes... But they’re not just any parautilitarians. They are Persons of Power. The crème de la crème.

Our goal is to locate these exceptional individuals and provide them with the most rigorous, comprehensive, and demanding training possible, shaping them into exactly what the Bureau needs.

But we’re not here merely to foster the growth of these extraordinary individuals. We cannot overlook this unique opportunity to further our own knowledge. Here we stand on the precipice studying the very limits of paranatural human ability!

Welcome to the team! It’s great to have you onboard.

(8/19)

Something Innate

Dr. Darling's log regarding Dylan's dangerous abilities, and how to control them.

I’ve been thinking about what to do. How to handle this... sticky situation. After Roberts died, most of the staff want nothing to do with Dylan. They’re afraid of him. And not without reason.

I told Roberts to be hard on Dylan, but... he went further than I realized. It’s possible Dylan, P6, saw it as self-defense. Regardless, we now know that he can’t control his abilities.

But the thing is, he really showed us something.

The cost was awful, too much, but even so... that potential he’s been teasing us with for so many years, now it’s there. And it wasn’t from binding an Object of Power, but something innate. Some kind of extradimensional event, maybe related to what we saw from him before.

The point is: further testing will be risky. But given Director Trench’s condition, it’s obvious we need to keep working. There are no alternative routes.

To continue testing safely, we need an insurance policy. A way to shut P6 down. We’re way beyond armed guards at this point. I think I have a plan.

P6 can do amazing, incredible, terrible things. What happened to Roberts proved that. I just have to figure out what those things are and how to shape them, without being a liability. If P6 can do this, what else might he be capable of? Both good, and bad.

When you first... When we first brought you in. I saw so... so much potential. The opportunity for you, The Bureau, for scientific advancement. You may not believe this, but I really thought we were going to make your life better. Ridiculous. It's ridiculous.

I’ll be leaving soon.

I just-- I have to take care of some things first.

I won’t waste your time with an apology we both know isn’t real. So.

But that's not what I wrote in my report.

I did what I had to do to protect the Bureau. This-- this mortal dimension. So, I had to lie to maintain my position.

You’re smart enough to understand that, Dylan. Right?

Trench is lost. I’m sure of that now. The situation is--

My actions were... unpleasant. Unethical, yes. Necessary.

The mistake I made was putting all my end-of-the-world-deterrence eggs into one basket. You were all I had so I-- I pushed until you broke.

You know, I always said I wouldn't be like my father. Well...

(10/19) NG+

More Qualified

Dr. Darling's thoughts on the Faden siblings' respective potentials as Prime Candidates.

I just had a meeting with Director Trench and he told me we have a fix on Jesse Faden. She’s quite slippery. Always on the move, evading us almost on instinct. But now that she’s on our radar, we can pick her up whenever we want to.

Except, we don’t want to according to Trench. He was evasive on the subject. He said we should observe her from afar. Wasn’t interested in debating the issue. He wants the two kept apart. To me, that feels like orders from above, which is interesting.

Regardless, this can work in our favor. Dylan, P6, is the experiment and Jesse, P7, will be the control. We shape every element of one life, while another is left to fend for itself. Now currently, she’s living in an abandoned apartment, supporting herself on petty theft. And her brother is enjoying meatloaf and Bureau-produced cartoons.

Jesse might end up being the tougher candidate. Maybe we should adjust our parameters to--Well.

In any case, it’s fairly academic. We’re training Dylan, so obviously, he’ll be the more qualified Prime Candidate. But it’s nice to have a backup, just in case.

(11/19) NG+

Failure

A late-night log from Dr. Darling, ruminating on Dylan's failures.

Long night parsing through data. Dylan is proving to be a frustrating test subject. Every attempt to tap into his power has been a failure.

Sometimes I wonder if the boy is even trying. Maybe he’s just not up to the task. I wish Trench would let us bring in the sister. If she has anything like Dylan’s potential, I mean, maybe she’d be less of a disappointment.

I’ve been looking into the official records from Ordinary. I can only go by what’s on paper since there’s no one left to interview, but there’s something about those two. The paper trail is spotty. It's incomplete. The Faden children only seem to exist in the town’s own records. Even on the county level. There's only a few mentions of them, and really only one, or they--their names were mixed up. It’s hard to nail down anything concrete about their childhood. I don’t want to ask P6 about it anymore. He doesn’t remember much, and the less time he spends obsessing about his sister the better. We want his focus on the here and now.

Oops. Supervisor meeting in fifteen.

Lost Cause

Dr. Darling's log following a severe incident during Dylan's training.

I've squared away the mess. There will be fallout but nothing I can’t manage. As for P6, well-- It’s a whole new level of security. No more experiments, throw away the key. He will be locked up until... Indefinitely.

I triggered the Mobile Containment Device but it was too late. Dylan's memory of the event is shot. But I couldn't risk it, I had to remove him. I couldn't have him disputing my report. My work is too important to be compromised now. Without Dylan, I'm the only one who can find another way.

He is in rough shape. He could use crisis counselling. But with his current level of security-- There's no point me drowning with him. Dylan is a lost cause. He couldn't be what I wanted him to be.

I lied in my incident report. There were mitigating circumstances. On the day of the event we had a mixup with his meds, it was a double dose of stimulants. It was stupid. It was stupid!

Look, I wanted him to be on edge. But at that dosage-- Look I knew something was off. P6, he was too tightly wound, he was too twitchy, he was too scared. Look, I should have called the whole thing off but I had a way to shut him down. It should have been-- It should have been fine! And he'd never been so intense before. I-- I thought maybe he... I thought maybe he would manifest!

Well, he manifested power alright. And good people died.
