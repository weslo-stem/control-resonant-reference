Something Innate

Dr. Darling's log regarding Dylan's dangerous abilities, and how to control them.

I’ve been thinking about what to do. How to handle this... sticky situation. After Roberts died, most of the staff want nothing to do with Dylan. They’re afraid of him. And not without reason.

I told Roberts to be hard on Dylan, but... he went further than I realized. It’s possible Dylan, P6, saw it as self-defense. Regardless, we now know that he can’t control his abilities.

But the thing is, he really showed us something.

The cost was awful, too much, but even so... that potential he’s been teasing us with for so many years, now it’s there. And it wasn’t from binding an Object of Power, but something innate. Some kind of extradimensional event, maybe related to what we saw from him before.

The point is: further testing will be risky. But given Director Trench’s condition, it’s obvious we need to keep working. There are no alternative routes.

To continue testing safely, we need an insurance policy. A way to shut P6 down. We’re way beyond armed guards at this point. I think I have a plan.

P6 can do amazing, incredible, terrible things. What happened to Roberts proved that. I just have to figure out what those things are and how to shape them, without being a liability. If P6 can do this, what else might he be capable of? Both good, and bad.
