Jesse Faden

(1/13)

I Have to Go

An echo of Jesse, reminiscing about her and Dylan's childhood.

It’s hard to explain, but I'm working on a plan. I think I can use the Slide Projector. Just like old times, right?

It’s... Do you remember that game console we got for Christmas one year, from Mom and Dad?

We would stay up all night playing ”Severed Circle 2”. And there was that one level, where the kids had to go to the realm in-between and save the world... That’s basically what I’m gonna do. Just need to find it first. And use the Slide Projector to get there.

Remember what Dad used to say, before things got bad? Whenever we felt like giving up? “Don’t stop at the hard part.” I’m gonna try not to, Dylan.

(2/13)

I Feel It

An echo of Jesse, explaining the choice she has to make.

I feel it every day now. An itch on my brain that I can't scratch. I don't know what it is yet. But it's not good.

It's like... There's an earthquake coming. You think you're prepared, but then it hits, and you lose everything anyway.

Except that wasn't the earthquake. It was a paperclip falling off a desk. The tiniest tremor before the main event. The paperclip's about to fall, Dylan. And after that... the real earthquake is coming next.

I don't know how bad it'll get. But I have to try to stop it.

When I leave, the lockdown will fall. I have to make a choice, between bad and worse. So many lives, or every life. There's no more time -- if I don't go now, it'll be too late. I'm sorry. I can't explain how, not even to myself, but I know the Hiss won't get far.

What's that saying? "You can't unscramble an egg."

(3/13)

Repeating, Refracting

An echo of Jesse, talking about her premonitions.

I don't feel like myself. It was just an annoyance at first. Like getting your sleeves wet washing your hands.

The time passes, and they never dry. You look again, and realize your hands are rotting now.

It's a cliche. A trope. Something seen a million times before. Repeating, refracting, like light in a kaleidoscope. But there is something wrong. You pick up your favorite book and realize it is not the story you thought it was at all.

Emily is worried. But I can't tell her what's happening to me. I could never explain, and there's nothing she could do anyway. My thoughts keep circling. I do the same thing every day and I don't notice. I think... it's connected to what's coming. It's not going to stop unless I stop it.

Ever had that feeling? When you think there's one more step at the bottom of the stairs?

I'm sorry that I can't be here when you wake up. Sorry that I have to go.

(4/13)

Before I Leave

An echo of Jesse, wishing things could have been different.

Arish says I spend too much time talking to you.

Before I leave, I need you to know, Dylan: I never stopped looking for you.

Sometimes I wonder, if we had ever met... if you’d still be my baby brother...

I'm sorry I was too late.

I'm sorry it took so long to find you.

(5/13)

I'm Sorry

An echo of Jesse, for the last time.

I'm sorry I couldn't save you from everything they put you through.

I wish I could say goodbye.
