Hiss


Drone Enemies

Hiss Guard

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS GUARD

-

This "Drone" enemy type has been observed attacking from a distance by using projectiles drawn from its surroundings, seemingly telekinetically.

-

The Hiss Guard looks for higher ground, possibly to gain a clearer view of its opponent. This suggests some tactical thinking although "thinking" might not be the correct word to use regarding the Hiss.

-

This enemy has better aim with a still target. Does this imply poor vision or poor coordination? Or is this Hiss variant simply impatient, hasty in its actions during combat?

-

Note that if you get too close to the Hiss Guard, it will use a direct melee defense. Or attack, depending on your viewpoint.

-

Relatively easy to defeat, especially in smaller numbers. Although, this statement is of course easy to make from the side-lines.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Charged

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS CHARGED

-

Yet another known variant from the Hiss invasion that overtook the Oldest House.

-

The Hiss Charged is a "Drone" enemy that boldly flies towards its target and, upon reaching it, self-detonates.

-

This self-sacrificing method of attack has of course been used widely in ordinary human warfare as well. There, it carries a more tragic connotation, but in the context of the Hiss, it is merely an efficient means to an end.

-

Due to its modus operandi, this enemy cannot be stunned. This Hiss variant also explodes upon death, making it a "living", flying landmine of sorts. Not pleasant.

-

The damage the explosions cause is significant and affects the immediate surroundings, and sadly, we've seen it leave people with severe burns, in the cases where they live.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Elevated

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS ELEVATED

-

This Hiss variant is familiar to us from the Oldest House invasion and seems to have remained in its original state even beyond the Lockdown.

-

The Hiss Elevated is a "Drone" enemy that moves around by levitating, while appearing to keep the target in its line of sight.

-

When airborne, the Hiss Elevated has been reported to deftly dodge some of our ranged attacks - a skill it has had plenty of time to practice.

-

I've observed an interesting attack from the Hiss Elevated where it charges the target at considerable speed and hits them with a small impulse wave. Although this sudden attack momentarily drains the enemy, leaving it vulnerable on the ground, it still exhibits this behavior. A lack of strategical thinking or an "all or nothing" mentality? Can the Hiss exhibit either?

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Provoker

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS PROVOKER

-

The Hiss Provoker is another "Drone" enemy that uses projectiles. but what makes this specimen especially interesting is its stationary nature

-

The enemy appears almost shy: it's encased within an armored shell to which it withdraws when its opponent comes near.

-

However, with enough force, the protective shell can be destroyed, so this Hiss variant is not fully safe inside the little bubble it creates for itself. This begs the question is this an early phase of an evolution that would lead to a fully cocooned specimen? (Lydia, I'd like to hear your thoughts on this!

-

The casing itself seems to be the fleshy remains of the person this Hiss enemy used to be, and the operating part of it now only the skeleton. Disturbing.

Hiss Passenger

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS PASSENGER

-

The Hiss Passenger, a fast and fairly small melee "Drone" enemy, seems to originate from the Hiss Transporter variant. This seems very logical, given their connection.

-

The enemy's appearance is that of a passenger of the corrupted bus, fused together with their seat. Gruesome.

-

I've observed this enemy using fast but simple melee attacks against its opponent when in close range. This methodology might indicate a specific "frustration" - perhaps due to the enemy being stuck to a chair, forever in transit, never arriving?

-

This Hiss variant seems to lack tenacity, especially when separated, which is likely why they often fight in packs. Or crowds, like real passengers tend to do.

-

Rangers who have encountered this enemy have stated that they were unable to stun the Hiss Passenger with their attacks, even when it was close to death.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Thug

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS THUG

-

The Hiss Thug is a melee "Drone" enemy that exhibits slow movement as it advances on its target, then an equally slow melee attack when in range.

-

To note, despite its apparent slowness, the Hiss Thug has been observed at times charging at the target and attempting an overhead attack. This move appears to happen when in medium range, possibly as an acute effort to overwhelm the opponent.

-

This Hiss variant uses its elongated, misshapen arms as weapons. One might wonder is this intentional, opportunistic action or just a physical flailing, a symptom of the mutation?

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Heavy Enemies

Hiss Transporter

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS TRANSPORTER

-

The large origin of the Hiss Passenger, the Hiss Transporter is a "Heavy" enemy that appears to have formerly been a city bus, only now corrupted and hovering above the ground. A peculiar variant.

-

This enemy is able to teleport when needed. Its motions are slow and almost avoidant as if this Hiss variant isn't so much interested in its opponent as it is in sending out its "spawn", the Hiss Passengers, to fight. An almost motherly dynamic.

-

In a pinch, the Hiss Transporter will defend itself by slamming its large body to the ground, which creates a shock wave around it.

-

I've observed our Rangers targeting the fused, former human bodies sticking out from the sides of the bus (an upsetting thing to see), and by destroying these parts of the enemy, being able to clearly weaken it.

-

The above seems to indicate that the people inside the vehicle at the time of corruption are now all one, a part of the whole. While there is some strange beauty in this unity, it's clearly also able to destroy the one, the self. A common theme with the Hiss, I suppose.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Collider

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS COLLIDER

-

A melee "Heavy" enemy with a standout appearance; tall stature with a large and extremely deformed head.

-

I've observed the Hiss Collider chasing its target quite slowly. then attacking them by slamming its head to the ground, using it almost like a large, fleshy mallet. It seems like this Hiss variant has little regard for its physical integrity as it uses its body/head brutally as a weapon.

-

It appears that when in mid-range, the enemy rushes at the target, plowing them and anyone else in its path down with its head. It's horrific, but hard to look away.

-

Gladly, I've witnessed some of our people being able to dodge or jump over the enemy when it is performing the above attack - much like a matador fighting a bull (a problematic "sport" but an apt comparison).

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Cluster

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS CLUSTER

-

The Hiss Cluster is an enemy we're already familiar with from the Oldest House invasion.

-

This Hiss variant is a "Heavy" type of enemy that does not participate in the battle directly, but appears to hide behind others and boost or even outright heal its nearby allies in the field.

-

With its spherical shape and its very different mode of operating, this enemy certainly stands out from the rest. A medic of sorts, the Hiss Cluster enemies seem to be the supportive troops to the more direct aggressors. Interesting.

-

Does the Hiss assign these different roles to individual Hiss variants according to some incomprehensible system or are they just born out of necessity there on the spot, like an improvised play? Your guess is as good as mine.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Distorted

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS DISTORTED

-

Another familiar face from our seven-year battle against the Hiss.

-

The Hiss Distorted is a "Heavy" enemy that is known to attack in close range with a conelike area of effect.

-

It can become nearly invisible, and use this to its advantage, following its target while in this advantageous state. Then, when close enough, it attacks, after which it quickly retreats again. This is a cunning stealthy strategy. This enemy certainly doesn't lack any drive or patience. Maybe it's even enjoying this game of cat and mouse?

-

I'd hate to be the mouse.

Hiss Riveter

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS RIVETER

-

The Hiss Riveter is another ranged "Heavy" enemy.

-

Its appearance reminds me of a deformed construction worker, complete with a jackhammer.

-

This Hiss variant will throw said construction tool at its opponent when they're close to the ground. Mere seconds after this, the jackhammer's pressure spikes and it explodes, unable to contain the energy within. I've observed this explosion burning the people caught in it.

-

The use of the tool as a weapon is interesting. Where other Hiss variants use themselves or their mutated body parts as weapons, this enemy carries a remnant of its former job and now uses it against others. There's some social commentary there - breaking free from the laborer's role, maybe?

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Ruiner

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS RUINER

-

The Hiss Ruiner is a large, flying "Heavy" enemy with a somewhat ghost-like appearance (at least, to my wild imagination). It has holes in its chest from which it fires beams of energy in groups of three, using different combinations.

-

I've discerned two other distinct attack styles with this Hiss variant. Both of these are more powerful than the above "simpler" attack, and will thus exhaust the enemy. One is what I call "Cruise" - the enemy flies over its target, firing explosive projectiles from its mouth, of all places. The other is what I call the "Dance" - the enemy fires all six of its energy beams at the same time while rotating around, almost like a rhythmic gymnast doing a pirouette with ribbons. While I might be making it sound elegant, it is a frightening sight.

-

This Hiss variant appears to have a weak spot on its back. I've seen our people take this enemy down by utilizing said vulnerability during combat, as the Hiss Ruiner exposes it when exhausted.

-

According to the Rangers' reports, the destruction of these weak spots takes three rounds to complete. Is three the magic number for any other Hiss variants? Something to look out for.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

Hiss Focused

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS FOCUSED

-

The Hiss Focused belongs to the ranged "Heavy" enemy group. This impressive variant uses a powerful beam of energy that tracks its target and pierces it upon contact.

-

The relentless tracking reminds me of a sniper - you don't want to get caught in its crosshairs.

-

I've observed this enemy being able to, quite nimbly. dodge some of our team's ranged attacks.

-

The way the Hiss Focused operates - from a distance, with accuracy and relentlessness - makes me wonder why this method is not more favored among the Hiss. Maybe the evolution of the Hiss is a work in progress and the different variants are test cases, experiments? Or maybe it's all randomized, meaningless? I really don't know.

Apex Enemies

Hiss Transporter - Apex

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS TRANSPORTER - APEX

-

One might think the Hiss Transporter does not require an "Apex" variant of itself, but alas, I've observed one in the field.

-

This enemy variant appears to have expanded its use of its "progeny", the Hiss Passengers. It no longer just spawns them, but uses them as projectiles. (I take back my earlier note on the Hiss Transporter's motherly approach.) (And yes, Lydia, I know what you're going to say.)

-

The Hiss Passengers that are used (and abused) in this manner fly straight at a target, hitting them impactfully, before resuming their regular chase behavior. At times, these makeshift projectiles even explode upon death. I'm not sure what to make of this change other than that the Hiss Transporter, and especially its "Apex" variant, seems to consider the Hiss Passengers completely expendable.

-

Interestingly, there appears to be an additional weak spot, a destructible "extremity" on this variant. Is this the cost of the "upgrade" to its M.O.? Something to think about.

Rick Patel
Senior Analyst, Threat Assessment Team

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HISS FREIGHTER - APEX

-

We have identified a powerful sub-type of some of the enemies to which we've given the moniker "Apex".

-

The "Apex" variant of the Hiss Freighter appears to use the same attack methods the Hiss Collider and the Hiss Freighter variants use namely, the "head slam" and the "plowing rush" attack, and the launch of projectiles from its head.

-

But from what I've observed about these more advanced variants, it seems like their attacks are notably enhanced. In this case, the "head slam" and "plowing rush" attacks are combined with challenging projectiles. Unfortunately, I have yet to see anyone survive.

-

This improved efficiency may suggest we're seeing the Hiss evolve in real time. An unnerving observation.
