Crush

Secondary Aberrant Form

A slow, high Damage attack that can knock enemies down. Can be charged to do more damage.

Charged attacks deal Falter.

Unlock Living Shard 2
Unlock Aberrant Strength Boost +5
Damage Type Blunt Damage
Power Recovery Medium
Critical Chance 0%


Damage and falter values will be increased by talents and status buffs. Below is a table of the base damage and falter values per Aberrant Strength level.
