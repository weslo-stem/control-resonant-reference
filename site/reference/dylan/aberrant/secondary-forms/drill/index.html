Drill

Secondary Aberrant Form

A quick attack. Charge to continuously drill the target.

Charge attacks deal Falter.

Unlock Living Shard 2
Unlock Aberrant Strength Boost +5
Damage Type Sharp Damage
Power Recovery Medium
Critical Chance 0%
