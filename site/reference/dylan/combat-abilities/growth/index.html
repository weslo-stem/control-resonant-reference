Growth

Combat Ability

Summon a Mold Ally that shoots spores at enemies. These spores build up Infected over time.

Obtained by defeating The Collective
Damage Type Mold Damage
Sharp Damage
Power cost 330
Maximum Mold Allies 2 (+1)


Maximum amount of Mold Ally summons can be increased to 3 by unlocking the talent Mold Colony. Damage and falter values will be increased by talents and status buffs. Below is a table of the base damage and falter values per Ability Strength level.
