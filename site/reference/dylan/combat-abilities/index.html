Combat Abilities

Astral Rebuke

Unleash a homing spike that erupts beneath enemies and throws them in the air. Applies Lifted and builds up Astralized.

Barrage

Sends projectiles hurtling towards a target.

Command

Take over an enemy's mind and turn them into an ally.

Ground Slam

Slams enemies from above. The area of effect and amount of Damage can be boosted if executed from higher up.

Growth

Summon a Mold Ally that shoots spores at enemies. These spores build up Infected over time.

Ignite

Melee Attacks and Barrage, Shield, and Volatile Anomaly do Fire Damage that applies Burn.

Inferno

Generates increasingly intense Fire waves applying Burn that builds up.

Push

Sends enemies flying backwards. Push them off ledges and into hazards for more Damage.

Shield

Shield absorbs damage. Its durability is equal to the Power meter.
Dodge with Shield to Shield Bash.

Spike

Summon an Astral Spike to attack enemies. It can also be directed to attack and knock down a target. Builds up Astralized.

Spore Burst

Rapidly fire Mold projectiles at a target, building up Infected.

Volatile Anomaly

Summon an ally that can be thrown and detonated - does more Damage if the ally has low health.
