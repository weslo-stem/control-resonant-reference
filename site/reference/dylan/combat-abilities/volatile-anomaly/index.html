Volatile Anomaly

Combat Ability

Summon an ally that can be thrown and detonated - does more Damage if the ally has low health.

Obtained by defeating The Artist
Projectile Damage Type Blunt Damage
Explosion Damage Type Blunt Damage
Summon Power cost 1000
Explosion Power cost 400


Inherits Fire Damage boost and Falter boost from Ignite. Damage and falter values will be increased by talents and status buffs. Below is a table of the base damage and falter values per Ability Strength level.
