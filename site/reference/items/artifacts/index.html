Artifacts

1996 Commemorative Coin

Health Elements will become more effective and restore a larger amount of Health.

Minted for an occasion no one can quite remember.

Amulet

Increases Critical Chance for all Melee attacks by +5%.

I don't know if this blessing can erase all my worries, but I'll take all the help I can get.

Badge 11-60

Dylan takes significantly less damage from Environmental Hazards.

The front of the badge says Fidelis Ad Mortem. Wonder what it means.

Bar Blade

Health regenerates slowly while a Summon is nearby.

It's basically being held together by that rubber band but still works like a charm.

Bout Guard

Adds +50% Power recovery but Dylan takes +20% more damage.

Wouldn't want to lose your teeth in a fight.

Brass Dusters

Combat Abilities deal +50% Damage but no Falter at all.

You could do some real damage with these.

Bug Spray

Adds +30% Damage and Falter against Mold enemies. However, Dylan receives twice as much Infected build-up.

Ordinary was always full of bugs in summer. The Oldest House sometimes got spiders.

Catnip Toy

Significantly reduces damage from falling into the void.

The smell is strong, maybe it'll get strays to like me.

Coffee Cup

Health regenerates slowly outside of combat.

I don't think I like coffee, but the researchers drank it all the time.

Compact

Critical Hits deal +50% more Damage. However, they deal 30% less Falter

I used to watch my mom put her makeup on all the time, before she stopped going out.

Energy Drink

Adds +30% Critical Chance for all melee attacks when Dylan is at maximum Health. Dylan takes +20% more damage while this Artifact is equipped

Darling let me have one of these to see if it affected my abilities. My hands were shaking for hours.

Face Mask

Gives Dylan resistance to Infected by increasing the time it takes for him to get the status effect.

Wearing one can protect you and others from all types of airborne illness.

Feeding Bottle

Adds +30% Power recovery. This benefit is applied while affected by Infected or Burn

Hopefully the parents had a backup bottle.

Fingerboard

Adds +25% Damage and Falter while using a Gravity Anomaly.

Jesse and I got one of these from a cereal box once.

Fork

Increases Dylan's Sharp Damage output by 15%.

Could be a pretty good weapon in a pinch.

Framing Hammer

Combat Abilities will deal +30% Falter but no Damage at all.

A heavy duty hammer used for construction. Standard issue.

Golden Earrings

Boosts Falter by +20%. However, Damage is decreased by -30%.

The stone in the center shines green and purple in the right light.

Heart Monitor

Prevents death and instantly regenerates Health to +20%. Breaks upon activation.

The tool of someone who saved many lives. I hope their life was saved in turn.

Lighter

Gives Dylan resistance to Burn by increasing the time it takes to get the status effect.

Jesse and I once found a lighter and a pack of cigarettes. We nearly coughed our lungs out.

Lit Sparkler

A Perfect Dodge will cause Dylan's next Dodge to create an explosive charge dealing Fire Damage to nearby enemies.

Is this going to burn a hole in my pocket?

Lost Prescription

Adds +30% chance to restore a small amount of Health after a Perfect Dodge.

It's practically empty, I hope they had a refill.

Medical Alert Bands

Adds +25% Falter to all attacks when Dylan's Health is less than 25%.

I really hope no one is missing these...

Molding Bar

Grants immunity to Infected and increases Damage and Falter against Mold enemies. Caps Health at 50%.

It's hard to resist eating it.

Mother's Locket

Adds +25% Damage when Health is less than 25%.

Even if they're far away, a mother keeps their child close. My mother had one of these.

Novelty Magnet

Nearby small resource crates are marked on the Radar.

These things are always way overpriced.

Old Subway Card

Significantly Increases Rush duration.

I wonder if I can still use this, maybe find some of Jesse's favorite spots in the city.

Ornate Buckle

Executing enemies recovers a large amount of Power. Damage taken is increased by 30%.

It's comically large, can anyone actually use this with a belt?

Paper Plane

Increases Power for every attack Dylan does while airborne.

I guess the articles weren't very interesting.

Pickpocket's Tool

The next Combat Ability after a Perfect Dodge costs no Power. This benefit doesn't stack.

Just enough to scare someone out of their valuables.

Practice Knot

Adds +15% extra Falter to attacks on Heavy enemies.

Knowing how to tie a good knot is the first step to becoming an expert survivalist.

Scrunchie

Increases Damage done by Primary Melee weapon forms by 10%.

Jesse had one just like this. She said it was lucky.

Shiny Pebble

Slowly restores Health up to 50% while using a Gravity Anomaly.

I used to love finding quartz and pretty stones to give to mom.

Spray Paint

Increases Dylan's Blunt Damage output by 15%.

Ordinary had some graffiti, but nothing like the stuff you see in New York.

Stellar Bracelet

When Shield is destroyed, it restores a small amount of Health but only when it's broken, not when it's cancelled.

It fits my wrist perfectly.

The Weekend Crossword

Increases Damage done by Combo Finishers by +15%.

Grandma loved these, said they used to keep her mind sharp.

Trusted Spanner

Increase Dylan's Raw Damage output by 15%

A wrench that's well worn and used.

Unfinished Lollipop

Increases Damage done by Secondary Melee weapon forms by 10%.

Smells like apple and... blueberry?

Wad of Cash

Makes the Command ability cost 1 fewer Command Charge. Minimum cost is still 1.

$182, to be exact.

Wallet

Combat Abilities consume Health when out of Power. Health cannot drop below 5% from this Artifact.

I should probably turn this in once this is over. I hope whoever it belongs to is still out there.

Water Pistol

Critical Hits deal +50% more Damage to Hiss Shield.

Jesse got one of these from a box of cereal when we were kids. She let me have it, so I could never bring myself to use it on her.

Whetstone

Adds +20% Damage for all Melee attacks and Combat Abilities, but Falter will be reduced by 20%.

Dad had one of these, too. He used it a lot to sharpen his hunting knife.

World Tour Tickets

Combat Abilities deal +100% more Damage. Power is capped at 50%.

I'd love to go see them someday, hope they haven't retired.

Worn Eraser

Gives Dylan significant resistance to all negative status effects by increasing the time it takes for them to build up.

If only all mistakes were easy to undo.
